My Icons

People who light the way.

"I train like I never won and I compete like I never lost."

Eileen Gu

She inspires me because she is extraordinary, she's a gold medalist at the olympics, a student at Stanford, fashion model and manage to sleep 10 hours a day! She proves that you can excel in a lot of aspects of your life and still maintain your health and sanity.

"You can't beat someone who's having fun."

Alysa Liu

She inspires me because she didn't give up on her love for her sport, came back from her retirement, went into the Olympics to have fun and enjoy herself, and she WON GOLD. This proves that time is just an illusion and that enjoying the process improves your performance without pressure.

"Do what scares you."

Christina Koch

She inspires me because she's a meaningful representation of women in STEM, overcame stereotypes and became the first woman to spend 328 consecutive days in space.
